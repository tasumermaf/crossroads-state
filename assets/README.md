# Original repository artwork

The banner and state diagram are original vector artwork generated by
`scripts/build_assets.py`. They contain only independently generated public
synthetic data and generic operation definitions. No source photograph, private
array, reserved reading, manuscript text, sacred-language content or historical
diagram is included.

- `banner.svg` is the canonical 1800 x 620 vector asset. The image shows the
  public `fixture_state(0)` as a 9 x 9 index table in an artistic isometric
  arrangement. Cell tint follows values above/below its mean; the green route
  is the actual synthetic ordered reader, and the gold marker is its reference.
  The presentation does not assert a physical embedding or a geometric theorem.
- `state-contract.svg` is a 1500 x 710 vector explanation of a generic four-cycle.
  Its values, readers, reference and exact inverse are computed by the public
  engine. `state-contract.json` records those inputs and outputs. The green
  badges 1 and 2 indicate reading order, not additional field values.
- The corresponding PNGs are 2x renders (3600 x 1240 and 3000 x 1420) for
  predictable display in repository front matter. Typography, colors and numbers
  are drawn into the original SVG; no raster image editing or external artwork
  was used.

Regenerate the vector assets with:

```sh
python3 scripts/build_assets.py
```

The committed PNGs were rendered from the corresponding SVGs in headless
Chromium at device scale 2. They can be regenerated with an SVG renderer of the
reader's choice. Minor font rendering differences do not alter the diagram's
data or operation. Asset licensing follows the owner's pending decision in
`LICENSE-DECISION.md`.
